# Sports Betting

## Overview

The platform supports sports betting across a wide range of sports and markets. Bets are placed using your wallet balance and settled once the event result is confirmed.

## Supported Sports & Markets

Sports coverage depends on your operator's configuration and the data feed providers in use. Commonly available sports include cricket, football, tennis, basketball, kabaddi, horse racing, and more. Markets within each event can include match winner, handicap, over/under totals, player performance, and many others.

## Understanding Odds

Odds represent the likelihood of an outcome and determine your potential payout. The platform typically displays odds in decimal format.

**Payout calculation**: Stake × Odds = Total Return (includes your original stake)

Example: A ₹100 bet at odds of 2.50 returns ₹250 total (₹150 profit + ₹100 stake) if it wins.

Odds change in real time based on market movements, especially for in-play (live) events.

## How to Place a Bet

1. Navigate to the Sports section and select a sport or event.
2. Browse the available markets for the event.
3. Click on the odds for your chosen selection. It will be added to your bet slip.
4. In the bet slip, enter your stake amount.
5. Review the potential payout and confirm the bet.

## Bet Types

**Single**: A bet on one selection. Wins if that selection is correct.

**Accumulator (Parlay/Multi)**: A single bet combining two or more selections. All selections must win for the bet to pay out, but the potential payout is multiplied across all selections.

**In-Play (Live) Betting**: Bets placed while an event is in progress. Odds update continuously to reflect the live state of the game.

## Bet Slip

The bet slip collects your selections before you confirm a bet. You can add multiple selections, switch between single and accumulator bet modes, and see the potential payout before confirming.

## Cashout

The cashout feature, where available, allows you to settle a bet before the event ends. The cashout value is calculated in real time based on current odds. Accepting cashout closes your bet immediately for the offered amount, regardless of the final result.

Cashout may not be available for all markets or at all times. It can be suspended during key moments of an event (such as a penalty kick in football).

## Bet Settlement

Bets are settled once the official result of an event is confirmed. Settlement is based on official sports data from licensed data providers. Winnings are credited to your wallet immediately after settlement.

In the case of discrepancies between data provider results and official results, the official governing body's ruling takes precedence.

## Voided Bets

A bet may be voided (refunded) in the following circumstances:

- The event is cancelled or abandoned before the minimum required play time is reached
- The market is settled incorrectly and corrected in a resettlement
- A technical error affected the bet at the time of placement

Voided bets are refunded to your wallet at the original stake amount.

## Bet Limits

Minimum and maximum stake amounts, as well as maximum payout limits per bet, are set by the platform and may vary by sport, market, and user account. These are enforced at the time of bet placement.

## Exposure

**Exposure always refers to sports betting.** When a customer asks about their exposure, it means the maximum potential payout the platform owes them across their open (unsettled) sports bets — in other words, the operator's financial liability if all those bets win.

Key points to know when answering exposure queries:

- **Exposure = maximum liability on open sports bets.** It is not a casino, Matka, or wallet balance concept.
- A player's exposure goes up when they place a new bet and down when a bet is settled (win or loss) or cashed out.
- High exposure on a market or player account may cause the platform to restrict further bets on that selection — this is normal risk management.
- If a customer says their exposure is "stuck", "not updated", or "showing wrong", it is a settlement/data feed issue on the sports side and should be escalated to the sports operations team.
- Exposure figures are typically visible in the player's bet history or open bets section under Sports.
