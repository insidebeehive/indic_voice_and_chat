# Bonuses & Promotions

## Overview

The platform supports several types of bonuses and promotions. Specific offers vary by operator. This article explains how the common bonus types work so you know what to expect when an offer is credited to your account.

## Types of Bonuses

### Welcome / First Deposit Bonus
A bonus credited after your first deposit, typically calculated as a percentage of the deposit amount (for example, 100% up to a certain amount). This bonus is usually credited automatically after a qualifying deposit.

### Deposit Bonus
An ongoing bonus credited on selected deposits. The offer terms (percentage, minimum deposit, maximum bonus amount) are defined per promotion.

### Losing Bonus / Cashback
A percentage of your net losses over a defined period (daily, weekly) is returned to your account as a bonus. This is calculated and credited automatically at the end of the qualifying period. The cashback is typically credited as bonus funds subject to a wagering requirement.

### Referral Bonus
A credit awarded when you refer a new player to the platform and they register and deposit. The bonus amount and conditions are defined by the referral program terms.

### Loyalty / VIP Rewards
Active players accumulate loyalty points or progress through VIP tiers based on their betting or wagering activity. Higher VIP levels unlock benefits such as higher withdrawal limits, dedicated support, enhanced odds, and periodic rewards.

### Reload Bonus
A deposit bonus available for returning players on subsequent deposits. Offered periodically or on specific days.

## Wagering Requirements (Rollover)

Most bonuses come with a wagering requirement, also called a rollover. This means you must wager the bonus amount (or the bonus plus deposit amount) a specified number of times before the bonus balance can be converted to real money and withdrawn.

**Example**: A ₹1,000 bonus with a 5× rollover requires ₹5,000 in qualifying bets before the bonus converts to withdrawable funds.

Bets placed with the main (real money) balance do not count towards bonus rollover unless specified in the offer terms. Casino games may contribute differently to rollover than sports bets — always check the specific offer terms.

## Claiming a Bonus

Most bonuses are credited automatically when the triggering condition is met (e.g., deposit made, referral completes). Some bonuses require you to opt in or enter a bonus code. The claim flow is shown at the time the offer is presented.

## Bonus Expiry

Bonuses expire if not used or if the wagering requirement is not completed within a specified time window. Expired bonuses and any remaining bonus balance are forfeited. The expiry period for each offer is stated in its terms.

## Bonus Balance vs. Main Balance

Your wallet may show separate main and bonus balances. When you place a bet, your main balance is used first. The bonus balance is used after the main balance is depleted, or as configured for specific games. Winnings generated from bonus-funded bets are credited to the bonus wallet until rollover is complete.

## Bonus Terms

Every bonus has specific terms covering:
- Minimum deposit or qualifying trigger
- Bonus percentage and maximum amount
- Wagering/rollover multiplier
- Qualifying games or bet types
- Expiry period
- Maximum bet size while a bonus is active

Always read the offer terms before opting in. Violation of bonus terms (such as placing maximum bets with bonus funds) may result in the bonus being cancelled.

## Opting Out of a Bonus

If you wish to forgo an active bonus, contact support. Removing an active bonus forfeits any remaining bonus balance and clears the wagering requirement.
