# Technical Help

## Common Technical Issues

### Page Not Loading / Blank Screen

- Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or pull-to-refresh on mobile).
- Clear your browser cache and cookies, then reload.
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari) or switch between app and browser.
- Check your internet connection. Switch between mobile data and Wi-Fi to isolate connectivity issues.
- If the issue persists across devices and networks, the platform may be undergoing maintenance. Check for any maintenance announcements.

### Login Issues

- Ensure you are entering the correct mobile number or email and password.
- Check that Caps Lock is not accidentally enabled.
- Use "Forgot Password" if you are unsure of your password.
- If you receive an "account suspended" or "account disabled" error, contact support.

### Game Not Loading

- Ensure your browser or app is up to date.
- Disable browser extensions or ad blockers, which may interfere with game scripts.
- Clear cache and cookies.
- Check that your internet connection is stable. Casino games and live streaming require a reasonably fast, stable connection.
- Try switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data or vice versa.
- Some games may not be available in certain regions or on certain devices.

### Deposit / Withdrawal Not Reflecting

- Wait a few minutes and refresh your wallet page. Most transactions process within minutes.
- For deposits: If the amount was debited from your bank but not credited to your wallet after 30 minutes, contact support with your bank transaction reference.
- For withdrawals: Check the withdrawal status in your transaction history. If status shows "Completed" but funds have not arrived, contact your bank, as processing times vary.

### OTP Not Received

- Check that you have network coverage (for SMS OTP) or access to your email.
- OTPs may take up to 2 minutes. Wait before requesting a new one.
- Check your spam/junk folder for email OTPs.
- Ensure the mobile number or email in your profile is correct.
- If the problem persists, contact support to verify your registered details.

### App Crashes or Freezes

- Force-close the app and reopen it.
- Ensure your app is updated to the latest version from the app store.
- Restart your device.
- If crashes continue, uninstall and reinstall the app.

## Browser Compatibility

The platform works best on modern browsers. Recommended browsers:

- Google Chrome (latest version)
- Mozilla Firefox (latest version)
- Safari (iOS 14+ / macOS Big Sur+)
- Microsoft Edge (latest version)

Internet Explorer is not supported.

## Supported Devices

The platform is accessible on:

- Desktop and laptop computers via web browser
- Android smartphones and tablets (via browser or app if available)
- iOS (iPhone and iPad) via Safari or app if available

Some game providers may have device-specific restrictions. If a game fails to load on your device, try on a desktop browser.

## Contacting Support

If the troubleshooting steps above do not resolve your issue, contact the support team. When you do, provide:

- Your registered mobile number or username
- A description of the issue
- Any error messages you saw
- Screenshots if possible
- The approximate time the issue occurred

This information helps the support team resolve your issue faster. Support contact details are available in the Help or Contact section of the platform.
