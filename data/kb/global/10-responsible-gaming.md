# Responsible Gaming & Self-Exclusion

## Our Commitment

The platform is committed to providing a safe and enjoyable gaming environment. Gambling is intended to be a form of entertainment. If it stops being fun or begins to affect your finances, relationships, or mental health, it is important to take action.

## Signs of Problem Gambling

It may be time to take a break or seek help if you notice any of the following:

- Spending more money or time on gambling than you intended
- Chasing losses by placing further bets to recover money lost
- Borrowing money to fund gambling
- Neglecting responsibilities, work, or relationships because of gambling
- Feeling anxious, irritable, or upset when not gambling
- Hiding your gambling activity from friends or family

## Tools Available to You

### Self-Exclusion

Self-exclusion allows you to voluntarily block access to your account for a specified period. During a self-exclusion period:

- You cannot log in to your account
- No marketing communications will be sent to you
- Any pending bonuses will be suspended

To activate self-exclusion, go to the Responsible Gaming or Account Settings section of the platform, or contact support. Self-exclusion requests are processed promptly.

Once a self-exclusion period begins, it cannot be reversed until the period expires. After the exclusion period ends, you may request account reactivation through support.

### Cooling-Off Period

A cooling-off period is a shorter break from the platform (e.g., 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days) without permanently closing your account. During a cooling-off period your account is temporarily suspended.

### Deposit Limits

You can set a limit on how much you deposit over a daily, weekly, or monthly period. Once you reach your limit, no further deposits are accepted until the period resets. Limit decreases take effect immediately; limit increases may have a waiting period applied.

### Bet / Loss Limits

Depending on the operator, you may also be able to set limits on the total amount you lose or wager within a given time period.

### Reality Check

A notification that reminds you how long you have been playing, helping you stay aware of the time spent gambling.

## How to Set Limits

Limits and self-exclusion options are accessible in the Responsible Gaming or Account Settings section after logging in. You can also contact support to request limit changes on your behalf.

## Getting Help

If you or someone you know is experiencing gambling-related harm, the following organisations offer free, confidential support:

- **iCall (India)**: Mental health support — iCall.tiss.edu
- **Vandrevala Foundation**: 1860-2662-345 (24/7)
- **iGaming Responsible Gambling helplines**: Listed by your operator in the platform's responsible gaming section

Speaking to a counsellor is a confidential first step. You are not alone.

## Underage Gambling

The platform is strictly for adults (18 years and above, or the legal age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a minor is accessing the platform, please notify support immediately.
