# Deposits

## How to Make a Deposit

To add funds to your wallet:

1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to the Wallet or Cashier section.
3. Select "Deposit".
4. Choose a payment method from the available options.
5. Enter the amount you wish to deposit.
6. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the payment.

Once the payment is processed, your wallet balance will be updated.

## Payment Methods

The payment methods available to you depend on your operator. Common options include UPI, bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS), payment wallets, and third-party payment gateways. The deposit screen will show only the methods that are active for your account.

## Deposit Limits

Minimum and maximum deposit amounts are set by your operator. These limits will be shown on the deposit screen before you confirm a transaction. If you attempt to deposit below the minimum or above the maximum, the transaction will not proceed.

## Processing Time

Most deposit methods are processed instantly or within a few minutes. Bank transfers may occasionally take longer depending on the bank. If a deposit is not reflected in your wallet after 30 minutes, check whether the payment was debited from your account. If it was, contact support with the payment reference or screenshot.

## Failed Deposits

A deposit may fail for the following reasons:

- Insufficient funds in your bank or payment account
- Bank or UPI transaction limit exceeded
- Payment gateway timeout
- Incorrect payment details entered

If a payment is deducted from your bank but not credited to your wallet, do not attempt the deposit again immediately. Contact support with the transaction reference, and the team will investigate and credit or refund accordingly.

## Deposit Fees

In most cases deposits are processed without any fees charged by the platform. However, your bank or payment provider may charge their own transaction fees. Check with your payment provider if you are unsure.

## First Deposit & Welcome Bonuses

If your operator offers a welcome or first-deposit bonus, it is typically credited automatically after your first qualifying deposit. Check the bonus terms for minimum deposit amounts and any other conditions.
