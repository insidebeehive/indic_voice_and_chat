# Matka & Lottery Games

## Overview

Matka is a traditional Indian lottery-style number game. The platform offers digital Matka and lottery games where players select numbers and win based on a published result draw.

## How Matka Works

Matka games are based on selecting one or more numbers from a defined range. Results are drawn at scheduled times and published on the platform. If your selected numbers match the drawn result, you win a payout based on the type of bet placed and the odds for that bet type.

## Common Matka Bet Types

**Single (Ank)**: Betting on a single digit (0–9). Pays out at a fixed multiple if that digit appears in the result.

**Jodi**: Betting on a two-digit combination (00–99). Higher payout odds since it is an exact pair match.

**Patti / Panna**: Betting on a three-digit result derived from the draw.

**Half Sangam**: A combination bet linking a single digit from one part of the draw with a Patti from the other.

**Full Sangam**: A combination bet linking two Pattis from both parts of the draw. Highest odds and rarest outcome.

The specific bet types available depend on the Matka market offered by your operator.

## How to Play

1. Navigate to the Matka or Lottery section.
2. Select the market (game) you wish to bet on.
3. Choose your bet type and enter your selected number(s).
4. Enter your stake amount.
5. Confirm your bet before the market closes.

Markets have a cut-off time before the result draw. Bets placed after the cut-off are not accepted for that draw and will appear in the next available draw or be cancelled, depending on platform configuration.

## Result Draws

Results are published at the scheduled draw time and are visible on the results page of the platform. Settled bets and winnings are reflected in your wallet immediately after the result is processed.

## Lottery Games

In addition to Matka, some operators offer structured lottery games where tickets are purchased for a fixed draw. The rules for lottery games are shown within the game itself, including draw times, ticket prices, and prize tiers.

## Winnings

Winnings are calculated based on your stake multiplied by the payout odds for the bet type. These are credited to your wallet automatically after result settlement.

## Cancelled Markets

If a Matka market or draw is cancelled for any reason, all bets on that draw are refunded to your wallet at the original stake amount.

## Open and Close Sessions

Each Matka market runs two independent draws per day — an **Open** session and a **Close** session. Each session has its own bid cutoff time and its own result declaration; the Open result and Close result are announced separately.

Some bet types can only be placed as Close-session bets because they depend on both draws to resolve — for example, a **Jodi** pairs a digit from the Open result with a digit from the Close result, so it can't settle until the Close draw happens. **Half Sangam**, **Full Sangam**, and **Red Bracket** bets work the same way.
