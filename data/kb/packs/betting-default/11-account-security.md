# Account Security

## Keeping Your Account Safe

Your account contains real money and personal information. Taking a few simple precautions greatly reduces the risk of unauthorised access.

**Use a strong, unique password.** Choose a password that is at least 8 characters long and includes a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid using your name, phone number, or common words.

**Do not share your credentials.** Never share your username, password, or OTP with anyone — including anyone claiming to be support staff. Genuine support representatives will never ask for your password.

**Log out on shared devices.** Always log out of your account when using a shared or public device. Do not save your password in the browser on devices you do not own.

**Secure your registered phone or email.** Your mobile number and email are used for OTPs and password resets. Keep these secure and update them promptly if you change them.

## One-Time Passwords (OTPs)

OTPs are used for login verification, password resets, and sensitive account actions. They are sent to your registered mobile number or email.

- OTPs expire within a few minutes — use them promptly
- Never share an OTP with anyone
- If you receive an OTP you did not request, it may mean someone attempted to access your account. Change your password immediately and contact support

## Suspicious Activity

If you notice any of the following, contact support immediately:

- Login from an unfamiliar location or device
- Transactions you do not recognise
- Unable to log in despite using the correct credentials
- Receiving OTPs you did not initiate

## Compromised Account

If you believe your account has been compromised:

1. Try to reset your password immediately using the "Forgot Password" option.
2. Contact support to report the breach and request a temporary account freeze.
3. Provide details of any unauthorised transactions so the support team can investigate.

The platform will not be liable for losses resulting from you sharing your account credentials with a third party.

## Account Closure

If you wish to permanently close your account, contact support. Closed accounts cannot be reopened. Any remaining balance will be withdrawn subject to standard verification requirements.

## Data Privacy

The platform stores your personal information and documents securely. Your data is used solely for account management, identity verification, and regulatory compliance. For queries about your data, contact support or refer to the platform's privacy policy.
