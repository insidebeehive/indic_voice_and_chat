# Wallet & Transactions

## How the Wallet Works

Your account wallet is a digital balance that holds the funds you have deposited on the platform. All bets, game stakes, deposits, and withdrawals go through your wallet. The wallet balance is shown in your account currency and is updated in real time as transactions occur.

Some platforms operate a multi-wallet model with a main (real money) balance and a separate bonus balance. Funds in the bonus wallet may have wagering requirements before they can be converted to real money or withdrawn.

## Types of Wallet Balances

**Main Balance (Real Money)**: Funds you have deposited or won from settled bets and games. These can generally be withdrawn subject to verification requirements.

**Bonus Balance**: Funds credited from promotional bonuses. These are typically subject to wagering or rollover conditions before they qualify for withdrawal. The bonus balance is used only after the main balance is exhausted during gameplay, or as configured by the platform.

## Transaction Types

The following types of transactions may appear in your wallet history:

| Transaction Type | Description |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Deposit | Funds added to your wallet from a payment method |
| Withdrawal | Funds transferred out from your wallet to a payment method |
| Bet Debit | Funds deducted when a bet or game stake is placed |
| Win Credit | Funds added when a bet or game round is settled in your favour |
| Bonus Credit | Funds added as a promotional bonus |
| Bonus Debit | Bonus funds used during gameplay |
| Cashback / Losing Bonus | Funds returned as part of a cashback or losing-streak promotion |
| Referral Credit | Funds credited for referring a new player |
| Commission Credit | Funds credited as agent commission |
| Internal Transfer | Transfers between your own wallets (e.g., main to bonus) |

## Viewing Your Transaction History

Your full transaction history is accessible from your account's wallet or history section. You can typically filter by date range and transaction type. For detailed queries on specific transactions, contact support with the transaction reference number.

## Currency

Your wallet operates in the currency assigned at registration. If the platform supports multiple currencies, your currency is fixed at the time of account creation and cannot be changed after the fact. All game results, bets, and promotions are calculated in your account currency. There is no workaround for this — registering a new or second account to obtain a different currency is not a valid alternative (it violates the platform's one-account-per-person terms) and should never be suggested as a solution.

## Real-Time Balance Updates

Wallet balances are updated in real time. After a deposit, game win, or bonus credit, your balance refreshes immediately. If you notice a discrepancy, wait a few minutes and refresh the page before contacting support.
