# Withdrawals

## How to Request a Withdrawal

To withdraw funds from your wallet:

1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to the Wallet or Cashier section.
3. Select "Withdraw".
4. Choose your withdrawal method and enter your bank or payment details.
5. Enter the amount you wish to withdraw.
6. Confirm the request.

Your withdrawal will be queued for processing. You will receive a notification once it is approved and transferred.

## Withdrawal Methods

Available withdrawal methods are determined by your operator and will be shown on the withdrawal screen. Withdrawals are typically processed back to the same bank account or payment method used for your deposit.

## Withdrawal Limits

Minimum and maximum withdrawal amounts apply and are set by your operator. These are shown on the withdrawal screen. If you have a balance exceeding the single-withdrawal maximum, you may need to make multiple requests.

## Processing Time

Withdrawal processing involves two stages:

1. **Platform review**: The platform processes and approves your withdrawal request, typically within a few hours to 24 hours.
2. **Bank transfer**: Once approved, the funds are transferred to your bank or payment account. Bank transfers may take an additional 1–3 business days depending on your bank.

## Requirements Before Withdrawing

The following must be met before a withdrawal can be processed:

- **KYC verification**: Your identity must be verified. Unverified accounts cannot withdraw.
- **Bank account details**: You must have saved valid bank account or UPI details in your profile.
- **Wagering completion**: Any active bonus wagering requirements must be met before those funds can be withdrawn. Real-money funds that are not subject to a bonus rollover can usually be withdrawn independently.
- **Minimum balance**: You must have at least the minimum withdrawal amount available in your main (real money) balance.

## Withdrawal Status

You can track the status of your withdrawal from the transaction history section. Common statuses include:

- **Pending**: Submitted and awaiting platform review
- **Processing**: Approved and being transferred to your bank
- **Completed**: Funds have been sent to your account
- **Rejected**: The request was declined (you will be notified of the reason)

## Rejected Withdrawals

A withdrawal may be rejected if:

- KYC is not completed
- Bank details are incorrect or do not match verified identity
- The account has an active bonus with unmet wagering requirements
- The requested amount exceeds available real-money balance

Rejected withdrawal amounts are returned to your wallet. Correct the issue and resubmit.

## Withdrawal Fees

Most withdrawals are processed without platform fees. Check with your bank if additional charges apply on their end.
