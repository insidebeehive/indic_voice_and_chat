# Account Registration & Login

## Creating an Account

To get started on the platform you need to register an account. Registration is open to new users who meet the eligibility criteria set by the platform. During sign-up you will be asked to provide basic personal details such as your name, mobile number or email address, and a password of your choosing. Some platforms may also ask for your date of birth or country of residence to verify eligibility.

Once you submit the registration form your account is created and you can log in immediately, although certain features (such as withdrawals) may require identity verification before they are unlocked.

Each mobile number or email address can only be linked to one account. Creating multiple accounts on the same platform is not permitted.

## Logging In

To log in, visit the platform and enter the mobile number or email address you registered with, along with your password. If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" option on the login screen.

For security reasons, your session will expire after a period of inactivity. You will be asked to log in again when this happens.

## Password Reset

If you cannot remember your password:

1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page.
2. Enter your registered mobile number or email address.
3. You will receive a one-time password (OTP) or reset link.
4. Follow the instructions to set a new password.

If you do not receive the OTP, check that you entered the correct mobile number or email and try again. OTPs expire within a few minutes, so use them promptly.

## Account Status

A newly created account may be in a pending state until basic verification steps are completed. If your account has been suspended or disabled, please contact the platform's support team for assistance.

## Single Account Policy

You are allowed only one account per platform. If you have lost access to an old account, contact support rather than creating a new one. Duplicate accounts may be merged or closed, and any balances may be affected.
