# KYC & Identity Verification

## What Is KYC?

KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It is a verification process that confirms your identity before you can access certain features of the platform, most commonly withdrawals. KYC is a standard requirement in the online gaming and financial industry to prevent fraud, money laundering, and underage access.

## Why Is KYC Required?

KYC protects both you and the platform. It ensures that funds can only be withdrawn to the verified account holder, prevents unauthorized use of your account, and keeps the platform compliant with applicable regulations.

## Documents Typically Required

You will generally need to submit two types of documents:

**Proof of Identity** — A government-issued photo ID such as:
- Aadhaar card
- PAN card
- Passport
- Voter ID
- Driving licence

**Proof of Address** (may be requested separately):
- Utility bill
- Bank statement
- Aadhaar card (if it shows your current address)

**Selfie Verification** — Some platforms require a selfie holding your ID document to confirm it is being submitted by the actual account holder.

## How to Submit KYC Documents

Log in to your account and navigate to the profile or verification section. Upload clear, high-resolution photographs or scans of the required documents. Make sure all four corners of the document are visible, the text is legible, and no details are obscured.

Poor-quality images are a common reason for KYC rejection. Retake photographs in good lighting if the first attempt is unclear.

## Verification Timeline

KYC documents are typically reviewed within 24 to 48 hours. You will be notified once your verification is complete or if additional documents are needed.

## What Happens After KYC Is Approved?

Once your KYC is approved, withdrawal restrictions linked to unverified status are lifted. Your account is marked as verified and the documents are stored securely.

## KYC Rejection

If your KYC is rejected, you will receive a reason. Common reasons include blurry or incomplete documents, mismatched names, or expired IDs. You can resubmit corrected documents.

## Privacy of Your Documents

All submitted documents are stored securely and are only used for identity verification purposes. They are not shared with third parties except where required by law.
